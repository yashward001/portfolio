## The challenge

At the [Singapore-India Hackathon 2026](https://news.smu.edu.sg/news/2026/11/18/singapore-india-hackathon-2026-names-four-winning-ideas-better-future), Singapore's Ministry of Trade and Industry set the Sustainability and Climate Action track. The task was to help Indian smallholder farmers improve the quality, consistency and reliability of their produce, so they can meet the needs of larger buyers, including Singapore importers.

Teams were mixed across both countries and had 36 hours to build.

## What we built

Farmora helps Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs) turn buyer demand into coordinated crop plans:

- **Plan land across farms:** allocate an exporter's order across member farms.
- **Match farmers to crops** using local weather and climate data.
- **Track supply:** record voluntary farmer responses and expected supply against each deal.
- **Reach farmers where they are:** guidance in each farmer's preferred language.
- **Give exporters visibility:** consented harvest outlooks let exporters find and connect with FPOs.

![Farmora's FPO workspace: live deals, an India weather-risk map and alerts that flag which shipments a cyclone or flood could disrupt.](/images/farmora-dashboard.jpg)

The demo shows an FPO's workspace with sample data: active export deals and their harvest progress, upcoming deliveries, and a state-by-state weather map. When a forecast threatens a deal, such as a cyclone near the port on a shipment's route, Farmora flags it with a suggested action, like booking a backup sailing.

## Result

Holy Crop won **first place** in the Sustainability and Climate Action track. SIH 2026 was the fourth edition of the hackathon, held at SMU from 15 to 17 November 2026. It was organised by SMU's Institute of Innovation and Entrepreneurship, India's All India Council for Technical Education and the India Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell, in collaboration with TiE Singapore.
